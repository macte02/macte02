### Featured project

**[Asuntovahti](https://github.com/macte02/asuntovahti): Finnish housing price estimation.**
CatBoost quantile models trained on 104,000+ completed sales give every home on the market a price range,
comparable sales and a confidence score. Tested on 10,400 later sales: **R² 0.92** on apartments and row
houses, and about 10% median error in Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa.
