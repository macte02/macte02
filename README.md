### Featured project

**[Housing price estimation model](https://github.com/macte02/asuntovahti)**: CatBoost quantile models
trained on 104,000+ completed Finnish home sales give every home on the market a price range, comparable
sales and a confidence score. Tested on 10,400 later sales: **R² 0.92** on apartments and row houses, and
about 10% median error in Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa.
